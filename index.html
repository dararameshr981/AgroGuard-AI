      🌱 About AgroGuard AI

      AgroGuard AI is a mobile-first software prototype for early crop disease and pest detection. It combines image analysis, crop context, weather risk and verified agricultural guidance.

      Hackathon architecture: Mobile PWA → AI vision service → disease knowledge base → weather service → recommendation engine → web dashboard.

      AgroGuard AI • SIH 2026 prototype • AI output is decision support, not a substitute for an agricultural expert or product label.
